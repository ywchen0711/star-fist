# Credits

## Art, music, effects and jingles

**Ninja Adventure Asset Pack** by [Pixel-boy](https://pixel-boy.itch.io/) and [AAA](https://www.instagram.com/challenger.aaa/), released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack

Credit isn't required, but it's gladly given. The game uses:

- character sprite sheets and portraits (`assets/chars`, `assets/face`);
- monster sheets (`assets/mon`) and boss idle strips (`assets/boss`); a few are recoloured in code;
- effect strips (`assets/fx`): explosion, sparks, rings, cuts, shields, smoke, lightning, boost and rocks;
- a few item icons (`assets/items`);
- twenty music tracks (`assets/music`) and four jingles (`assets/jingle`), renamed by where they play.

`assets/LICENSE-NinjaAdventure.txt` is the pack's own licence.
